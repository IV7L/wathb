# وَثْب — Wathb Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Arabic name:** وَثْب  
**English name:** Wathb  
**Tagline (Arabic):** خطوتك للشغل الجاي.  
**Tagline (English):** Your step toward tomorrow's work.

Wathb (وَثْب) means *leap* in Arabic. The brand embodies momentum, forward motion, and the daily dignity of work in Gaza. Every design decision should reinforce: this platform helps people leap toward opportunity.

---

## Logo System

The Wathb visual identity has four components:

1. **Symbol** — A leaping human figure over a teal wave with a gold sun. See `assets/wathb-symbol.svg`.
2. **Arabic wordmark** — وَثْب in bold weight. See `assets/wathb-wordmark-ar.svg`.
3. **English wordmark** — Wathb in matching weight. See `assets/wathb-wordmark-en.svg`.
4. **Lockup** — Symbol + Arabic wordmark + English wordmark combined. See `assets/wathb-lockup.svg`.

### Logo Usage Rules

- **Minimum clear space:** Equal to the height of the letter ث on all sides of the lockup.
- **Minimum size:** 32dp height on screen; 12mm height in print.
- **Do not** recolor the symbol's gold sun or alter the teal/gold ratio.
- **Do not** place the full-color logo on dark backgrounds without using the light-on-dark variant (white figure + gold sun on teal).
- **Do not** stretch or skew any logo component.
- **Do not** add drop shadows to the wordmark.

### Preferred Placement

- Full lockup: login screens, splash screens, marketing materials.
- Symbol only: app launcher icon, favicons, small badges.
- Wordmark only: document headers when context is clear.

## Voice and Tone

- **Direct and human.** Short sentences. No bureaucratic language.
- **Optimistic but grounded.** Gaza's reality is hard; the platform acknowledges this without dwelling on it.
- **Arabic-first.** All UI copy is Arabic. English is secondary (developer docs, API).
- **Formal enough for business, approachable enough for workers.**

### Copy Examples

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| "تعذّر إتمام العملية بسبب خطأ غير متوقع" | "حدث خطأ. حاول مجدداً." |
| "المستخدم غير مخوّل بالوصول" | "انتهت جلستك. سجّل الدخول من جديد." |
| "تم استلام طلبك بنجاح" | "تم التسجيل!" |
